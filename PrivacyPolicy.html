Use of cookies

A cookie is a short text file that a website sends to your browser... Cookies serve a variety of purposes. For example, we use them to store your settings or to track the number of visitors to a website. After you have given your consent, so-called tracking cookies will be used. Without your consent, only technical cookies will be used. Changing your consent You can call up the offer to grant/withdraw your consent to the use of cookies again by deleting the cookies in your browser and reloading the page.
